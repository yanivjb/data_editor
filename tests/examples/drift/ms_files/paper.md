# Drift and sweeps

## Results

Mean time to loss or fixation increased roughly linearly with population size (Table 1, Figure 1), from 138.6 generations at N = 50 to 1150.5 at N = 400 (400 replicates each).

Table 1. Time to loss or fixation (generations).

| N | Mean | SD |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 138.6 | 110.1 |
| 100 | 262.3 | 186.9 |
| 200 | 557.9 | 402.3 |
| 400 | 1150.5 | 905.2 |

In SLiM, beneficial mutations (s = 0.05) fixed in 412 generations on average across 1000 replicate sweeps (Figure 2).
