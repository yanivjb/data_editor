\section*{Methods}
We measured sprint speed of 40 fringe-toed lizards (\emph{Acanthodactylus}, listed as Endangered) at GPS-located capture points in coastal dunes (Figure 1).
Speeds were compared between habitats with a permutation test (10,000 permutations) in Python, and with a one-way ANOVA in GraphPad Prism.
\section*{Results}
Lizards on rock ran faster than on sand (2.07 vs 1.79 m/s; difference 0.28 m/s, permutation $p = 0.006$; Figure 2).
The ANOVA agreed ($F_{1,37} = 8.9$, $p = 0.005$).
